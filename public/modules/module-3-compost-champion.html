2 Khaad Kaise Banti Hai? 🧪

Composting ek prakritik prakriya hai jisme sukshm jeev (micro-organisms aur bacteria) geele kachre ko poshtik kaali khaad mein badal dete hain. Iska ek saral formula hai:

✨ Composting Ka Super Formula ✨
GREENS (Gila Nitrogen) 🍌 + BROWNS (Sukha Carbon) 🍂 + Hawa (Oxygen) 💨 + Nami 💧 = KHAAD (Kaala Sona) 🌱
⚖️ Golden Rule: Thoda zyada Browns (60%) aur thoda kam Greens (40%). Agar Browns zyada honge toh badbu KABHI nahi aayegi!
📺 Training Video: Dekhiye ghar par aasan tarike se bina badbu ke khaad banane ka live process.

3 Kya Daalein? (Compost Friendly) ✅

Apne compost bin mein yeh saari cheezein khushi-khushi daal sakte hain:

  • ✅ Sabzi aur falon ke chhilke 🍌: Taze chhilke bacteria ke liye shandar khana hain.
  • ✅ Chai patti (dhoyi hui) 🍵: Cheeni aur doodh ko dho kar chhan lijiye, yeh best nitrogen deti hai.
  • ✅ Ande ke chhilke (pise hue) 🥚: Pees kar daalne se mitti ko calcium milta hai.
  • ✅ Sukhe patte 🍂: Bagiche ke sukhe patte moisture sokhte hain aur hawa banaye rakhte hain.
  • ✅ Thoda cardboard 📦: Bina ink wala brown gatta ya raddi kaagaz ke tukde.

4 Kya NA Daalein? (Strictly Avoid) 🚫

Compost pot ya bin mein yeh cheezein galti se bhi mat daaliye:

Cheez ❌ Kyun Nahi Daalna Chahiye? ⚠️
Tel / Ghee / Makhan wala khana Gandi badbu aati hai, hawa ruk jaati hai aur keede/makkhiyan aate hain.
Meat / Machhli / Haddi Sadne par bhayankar smell aati hai aur chuhe aur aavara janwar aate hain.
Doodh / Cheese / Dairy products Khatarnaak fungus aur harmful bacteria paida hote hain jo khaad ko kharab karte hain.
Spicy cooked food (Tala hua/Masaledar) Masale aur namak composting ke acche micro-organisms ko maar kar process slow kar dete hain.

5 Kis Paudhe Ko Kya Pasand Hai? 🪴

Alag-alag kitchen waste paudhon ko alag-alag zaroori poshak tatva (nutrients) dete hain:

Waste (Kachra) Kiske Liye Best Hai Kya Fayda Milta Hai
Kele ka chhilka🍌 Phool aur fal wale paudhe (Gulab, Tamatar) Potassium milta hai jisse khoob saare phool khilte hain
Ande ka chhilka🥚 Tamatar, baingan aur mirch ke paudhe Calcium milta hai jisse fal niche se sadte nahi
Chai patti🍵 Gulab aur acidity pasand karne wale gamle Nitrogen milta hai aur pattiyaan chamakdar hari hoti hain
Sabzi ki mix khaad🥬 Sabhi tarah ke bagiche aur gamlon ke paudhe Sampoorn poshan (General NPK) aur mitti ki nami bani rehti hai

6 Bacha Khana Waste Na Karo 🍲

Khana kachra nahi hai! Bacha hua saaf khana dustbin ya compost mein daalne se pehle hamesha kisi zarooratmand ya local Food Bank tak pahunchane ka prayas karein.

🌍

Climate Impact Fact

1 kg food waste bachana = lagbhag 2.5 kg CO₂ gas ka pradushan kam karna! Khana bachana aur compost banana dono humare environment ki sabse badi sewa hai.

💡 Myths vs Facts

Myth (Galat Fahami) ❌ Fact (Sacchai) ✅
Myth: "Ghar par khaad banane se poore mohalle mein gandi badbu aati hai." Fact: Sahi mix (zyada browns + kam greens + hawa) rakhne par bilkul koi badbu nahi aati, balki mitti jaisi meethi khushbu aati hai.
Myth: "Khaad banne mein ek poora saal lag jaata hai." Fact: Chhote tukde karke mix karne se khaad sirf kuch hafton (6-8 hafte) mein bankar taiyar ho jaati hai!
🧑‍🏫 Teacher & Parent Note

Ek chhote gamle ya plastic container mein thode sukhe patte aur kele/sabzi ke chhilke daal kar ghar pe mini-composting try karein. Bachon ko har hafte usme mitti banne ki prakriya ko observe karne dein!

📝 Compost Champion Quiz

5 sawaalon ke sahi uttar de kar apna Compost Champion badge unlock karein!

1. Inme se compost bin mein kya KABHI nahi daalna chahiye?
💡 Explanation: Meat aur machhli sadne par tez badbu, chuhe aur harmful keede aate hain, ise compost mein nahi daalte.
2. Kele ka chhilka khaas karke kis tarah ke paudhon ke liye sabse faydemand hai?
💡 Explanation: Kele ke chhilke mein Potassium bharpoor hota hai jo phool aur fal khilane mein madad karta hai.
3. Composting formula mein 'Browns' (sukha kachra) mein kya aata hai?
💡 Explanation: Sukhe patte aur bina print wala cardboard Carbon (Browns) provide karte hain.
4. Bina badbu ke acchi khaad banane ke liye kaisa ratio hona chahiye?
💡 Explanation: Browns thoda zyada (lagbhag 60%) rakhne se nami control rehti hai aur bilkul badbu nahi aati.
5. Bacha hua saaf khana fekne se pehle sabse pehle kya karna chahiye?
💡 Explanation: Saaf khana kachra nahi hai; ise pehle zarooratmand ya Food Bank tak pahunchana sabse behtar tareeka hai.
