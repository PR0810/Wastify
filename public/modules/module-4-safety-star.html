1 Hazardous Waste Kya Hai? ⚠️

Hazardous Waste (Khatarnaak Kachra) woh kachra hai jo zehreela (toxic), aag pakadne wala (flammable), chot pahunchane wala (sharp/cutting), ya bimari failane wala (infectious/biohazardous) hota hai.

Yeh normal sukhe ya geele kachre jaisa nahi hota. Agar yeh normal dustbin mein chala jaye, toh safai karmchariyon ki jaan ko khatra hota hai, aur yeh mitti tatha bhoomi-gat paani (groundwater) ko saalon tak ke liye zehreela kar deta hai.

2 Examples of Hazardous Waste

Humare gharon aur aas-paas aamtaur par milne wale hazardous items ko pehchano:

🔋 Battery / Cell
Isme acid, lead aur cadmium jaise toxic metals hote hain jo sadte nahi hain.
💊 Expired Dawai
Expired antibiotics aur syrups pani mein ghul kar poisonous ban jaate hain.
💉 Syringe & Needles
Used needles se chot lagne par hepatitis ya tetanus ka serious risk hota hai.
🌡️ Thermometer (Paara)
Isme liquid Mercury (paara) hota hai jo thoda sa bhi touch hone par zehreela hai.
🎨 Paint / Chemical
Paints, thinners, pesticides aur floor cleaners aag aur toxic gas chhodte hain.
💡 Bulb / Tubelight
Tubelight aur CFL mein mercury vapor aur kaanch ke tez pieces hote hain.
🔌 Charger / E-waste
Purani wiring, chargers aur electronics mein heavy metals maujood rehte hain.
🩺 Sanitary / Medical
Bandages aur medical cotton infectious bacteria aur virus failaate hain.

3 Ye Ghar Pe Recycle NAHI Karte ❌

⚠️ DANGER: In 3 Cheezon Ka Sakht Parhez Karein

  • 🚫 KABHI JALAO NAHI: Hazardous kachre ko jalane se jaanleva dioxin aur poisonous fumes nikalti hain jo phephadon ko damage karti hain.
  • 🚫 KABHI NALE / MITTI MEIN MAT DAALO: Nale ya mitti mein daalne par yeh bhoomi-gat jal mein mil jaata hai aur peene ke paani ko zehreela kar deta hai.
  • 🚫 NORMAL DUSTBIN MEIN NAHI: Neele ya hare dabbe mein daalne par safai karne wale safai-mitron ko deep cuts lagte hain aur infection ho jaata hai.

4 Safe Rules ✅

🛡️ Suraksha Ke Golden Rules: Inka Paalan Karein

  • 👉 Haath se mat uthao: Kisi bhi tuti hui glass, battery ya syringe ko nange haathon se kabhi mat chhuo.
  • 👉 Bade ko batao: Aisa koi bhi khatarnaak kachra dikhe toh seedha mummy-papa ya teacher ko inform karo.
  • 👉 Alag dabbe/bag mein rakho: Purani batteries aur dawaiyon ko ek alag safe container ya card-box mein isolate karke rakho.
  • 👉 Laal dabba use karo: Hazardous waste ke liye hamesha Laal Dabba (Red Bin) ya safe drop-off istemal karo.

5 Kahan Do? 📍

Hazardous waste ko kahan submit karna chahiye taaki yeh safe tarike se treat ho sake?

🏭
E-waste Collection
Purane phones, batteries, bulbs aur chargers authorized e-waste kendra par dein.
🏥
Medical Waste Bin
Clinics aur pharmacy ke bahar lage yellow/red biohazard bins mein dawai aur syringe dein.
🏛️
Nagar Nigam Facility
Municipality ki hazardous waste pickup drives aur safai kendra par dispose karein.

7 Myths vs Facts 💡

Myth (Galat Fahami) ❌ Fact (Sacchai) ✅
Myth: "Purani battery dustbin mein fekna theek hai." Fact: Battery mein cadmium, acid aur lead hota hai jo mitti aur groundwater mein milkar cancer aur bimariyan failata hai.
Myth: "Bachi hui dawai ko toilet mein flush kar do." Fact: Dawai flush karne se city water supply contaminate hoti hai aur jal-jeev (aquatic life) khatam ho jaate hain.
Myth: "Toota CFL bulb raddi wale ko de sakte hain." Fact: CFL aur tubelight mein zehreela Mercury vapor hota hai; ise special hazardous collection mein hi dena chahiye.
🧑‍🏫 Teacher & Parent Note

Ghar mein purani battery aur expired dawaiyon ke liye ek alag cardboard dabba banayein. Bachon ko sikhayein ki road ya ghar par koi bhi battery, syringe ya toota kaanch dikhe toh use chhue bina turant kisi bade ko inform karein.

📝 Safety Star Quiz

5 sawaalon ke sahi uttar de kar apna Safety Star badge unlock karein!

1. Inme se hazardous waste kaun sa hai?
💡 Explanation: Expired dawai chemical zeher hai aur hazardous waste category mein aati hai.
2. Agar road pe used syringe dikhi, toh kya karna chahiye?
💡 Explanation: Syringe se fatal infections ho sakte hain. Hamesha bade ko batao, nange haath kabhi mat lagao.
3. E-waste (jaise purana charger/battery) kahan dena chahiye?
💡 Explanation: E-waste ko certified e-waste collection centre par safely dismantle kiya jaata hai.
4. Hazardous waste ke sath kya KABHI nahi karna chahiye?
💡 Explanation: Hazardous kachre ko KABHI nahi jalana chahiye kyunki isse deadly toxic gas nikalti hai.
5. Laal dabba (Red Bin) kis tarah ke kachre ke liye hota hai?
💡 Explanation: Laal dabba hazardous/biomedical kachre ke liye alag se nirdharit hota hai.
