Module 5 · Core Principles

🔁 3R Master

Reduce, Reuse aur Recycle ke priority mantra ko samjhein. Jaano ki kyun sabse behtar kachra wahi hai jo kabhi bana hi na ho!

1 3R Kya Hai?

3R ka matlab hai Reduce (Kam Karo), Reuse (Dobara Istemal Karo), aur Recycle (Punar-chakran Karo).

🎯

Yeh Ek Priority Order Hai:

Pehle sabse zaroori hai Reduce karna. Agar cheez bach jaye toh use Reuse karein. Aur jab dono sambhav na hon, tab aakhri raaste ke roop mein Recycle karein!

Zyadatar log sochte hain ki recycle karna hi kafi hai, par sach yeh hai ki recycling mein bhi bijli, paani aur resources lagte hain. Isliye Reduce hamesha No. 1 priority hota hai.

2 REDUCE 🔽 (Kam Kharido, Kam Waste Banao)

Kachra management ka sabse aasan niyam hai: "Zaroorat kam, kachra kam!" Single-use cheezon ko na bolna hi Reduce hai.

🛍️
Jhola Saath Le Jao

Market jaate waqt kapde ka bag rakhein taaki polythene lene ki zaroorat hi na pade.

💧
Reusable Bottle Use Karo

Plastic water bottles khareedne ki jagah apni steel ya durable bottle carry karein.

📦
Kam Packaging Waala Samaan

Excess plastic layers aur wrapping waale items lene se bachein; bulk/loose options chunein.

📄
Paper Ke Dono Taraf Likhein

Ek taraf printed paper ki dusri taraf rough calculations aur notes ke liye istemal karein.

3 REUSE ♻️ (Dobara Istemal Karo)

Kisi cheez ko dustbin mein daalne se pehle usko ek naya kaam do. Isse naye resources nahi lagte aur cheezon ki life lambi hoti hai.

🪴
Khali Jar = Pencil Stand

Jam ya pickle ke khali glass jars ko paint karke pen stand ya chhota planter banayein.

🧹
Purana T-shirt = Dusting Cloth

Purane cotton kapdon ko phenkne ki jagah ghar ki safai aur dusting ke liye use karein.

💧
Purani Bottle = Plant Spray

Khali spray bottles mein paani bhar kar plants ya window cleaning ke liye reuse karein.

📦
Cardboard Box = Storage

Delivery waale gatte ke dibbon ko organize karke khilaune ya kitabein rakhne mein layein.

4 RECYCLE 🔄 (Aakhri Raasta)

Jab kisi item ko reduce ya reuse karna sambhav na ho, tab use alag karke recycling factory bhejna chahiye taaki usse naya raw material ban sake.

📰
Newspaper Raddi Wale Ko

Purane akhbaar aur notebooks raddi wale ko dein taaki recycled cardboards aur paper ban sake.

🧴
Plastic Saaf Karke Bin Mein

Milk pouches aur containers ko dho-sukha kar dry waste bin mein daalein.

🍶
Kaanch Alag Rakho

Glass bottles ko toote bina alag collect karein; glass ko anant baar recycle kiya ja sakta hai.

🥫
Metal & Tin Cans

Cold drink cans aur food tins metal recyclers ke paas jakar naye parts banate hain.

5 3R Pyramid 🏔️

The Waste Hierarchy Pyramid
Pyramid mein neeche zyada waste, upar kam — goal hai upar jaana!
🔽 1. REDUCE (Top Priority - Kam Banao)
♻️ 2. REUSE (Middle Tier - Dobara Use Karo)
🔄 3. RECYCLE (Base Tier - Aakhri Raasta)
⬆️ Hamara Lakshya: Base se Top tak pahunchna (Zero Waste Mindset)

6 Bachon ki Daily Life Mein 🎒

Aap apne school aur ghar mein har roz 3R champion ban sakte hain:

🍱
Tiffin Box = Reuse

Steel tiffin ka istemal karein; disposable foils ya plastic wraps bilkul na lagayein.

📓
Purani Notebook = Naya Cover

Pichle saal ki notebook ke bache hue blank pages jodkar rough copy banayein.

🚰
Water Bottle Refill Karo

School ke filtered coolers se baar-baar bottle bhar kar single-use paani ki botal na kharidein.

🎁
Gift Wrap Dobara Use Karo

Birthday gifts ke wrapping paper ko saaf tarike se kholkar agle gift ke liye sambhal kar rakhein.

7 Myths vs Facts 💡

Myth (Galat Fahami) ❌ Fact (Sacchai) ✅
Myth: "Sirf kachra recycle karna hi kaafi hai." Fact: Reduce sabse important hai; recycle aakhri option hai kyunki recycling process mein bhi bijli aur paani lagte hain.
Myth: "Ek baar use hone wali plastic chalti hai agar use recycle karein." Fact: 90% se zyada single-use plastic recycle nahi ho paati. Reusable items hamesha zyada eco-friendly aur saste padte hain.
Myth: "Purani cheezon ko phenkna hi naye zamane ka sign hai." Fact: Upcycling aur reuse karna sabse badi creativity hai jo nature ka bojh kam karti hai.
🧑‍🏫 Teacher & Parent Note

Is hafte apne ghar aur class mein aisi 3 cheezein dhundein jinhein aap reduce ya reuse kar sakte hain. Bachon ke saath milkar ek 'Reuse Station' box banayein jismein craft paper, ribbons aur dharne wale dibbe ikkathe hon.

📝 3R Master Quiz

5 sawaalon ke sahi uttar de kar apna 3R Master badge haasil karein!

1. 3R priority hierarchy mein sabse pehle kya aana chahiye?
💡 Explanation: Reduce hamesha pehla aur sabse prabhavi kadam hai taaki kachra paida hi na ho.
2. Inme se 'Reduce' ka sahi example kaun sa hai?
💡 Explanation: Jhola le jaane se naye plastic bags ka nirman aur istemal kam hota hai (Reduce).
3. Khali glass jar ka sabse accha reuse kya ho sakta hai?
💡 Explanation: Khali glass jar ko pencil stand ya plant pot bana kar dobara istemal (Reuse) kiya jaata hai.
4. Kisi cheez ko Recycle kab karna chahiye?
💡 Explanation: Recycle hamesha tisra aur aakhri vikalp hota hai jab cheez ko reuse karna asambhav ho.
5. 3R pyramid mein sabse neeche (base) par kaun sa step hota hai?
💡 Explanation: Pyramid ke aadhar (base) par Recycle hota hai, jisse bachkar hume top par (Reduce) jaana hota hai.
