1 Dry Waste Kya Hai? 📦

Dry Waste (Sukha Kachra) woh kachra hai jo sukha, saaf aur sadta nahi hai. Ise factory ya recycling plants mein bhej kar dobara upyog (reuse ya recycle) kiya ja sakta hai.

2 Recycling Kaise Hoti Hai? ⚙️

Jab hum sukha kachra alag karke dete hain, toh recycling factories mein alag-alag materials ko naye roop mein badla jaata hai:

Material Kaise Recycle Hota Hai Kya Banta Hai
Kaagaz📄 Paani mein bhigo kar pulp (luggdi) banate hain aur press karte hain Naya kaagaz, carton boxes, egg trays
Plastic🧴 Saaf karke flakes mein kaat kar pighlaate hain Nayi bottle, kapda/polyester fiber, garden bench
Kaanch🍾 Chhote tukdon (cullet) mein tod kar pighlaate hain Naya kaanch (quality hamesha same rehti hai!)
Dhaatu🥫 Bhatti (furnace) mein pighla kar moulds mein dhalte hain Naye metal cans, cycle frame, car ke parts
Kapda👕 Shred karke reshe (fibers) alag karte hain Cloth carry bag, mattress padding, rags
Lakdi🪵 Chhote chips aur mulch mein peeste hain Pressboard, wood panels, compost covering

3 Kaanch Ki Khaas Baat ✨

💎
Kaanch Ki Superpower!
Kaanch (Glass) BAAR BAAR (infinite times) recycle ho sakta hai — iski purity aur quality kabhi kam nahi hoti! 1 recycled glass bottle bachati hai itni urja jisse ek bulb 4 ghante jal sake.

4 Reduce & Reuse Pehle! 🔺

Recycle karna acchi baat hai, lekin 3R pyramid batata hai ki recycle se pehle do aur cheezein sabse zaroori hain:

The 3R Priority Pyramid
Upar ka step sabse pehle aur sabse mahatvapurna hai
🥇 1. REDUCE (Kachra Kam Paida Karo)
🥈 2. REUSE (Dobara Upyog Karo)
🥉 3. RECYCLE (Naye Roop Mein Badlo)
💡 Pro Tip: Recycle karne mein bhi factory mein urja aur paani lagta hai, isliye recycle karne se pehle hamesha cheezon ko dobara use karein ya kachra kam paida karein!

5 Ghar Pe Kya Karein? 🏠

Ek sacche Recycling Hero ki tarah apne ghar par yeh 4 aasan aadat apnao:

  • ✅ Dry waste saaf rakhkar alag karo: Neele dustbin mein sirf sukha kachra rakho taaki woh ganda na ho.
  • ✅ Raddi wale (kabadiwala) ko do: Purane akhbaar, cartons aur plastic bottle raddi wale ko do jo sidha recycling factory bhejte hain.
  • ✅ Plastic dhoke rakho: Dudh ke packet ya dahi ke dabbe ko halka paani se dho kar sukha kar rakhein. Ganda plastic recycle nahi ho pata.
  • ✅ Purane kapde donate karo: Jo kapde chhote ho gaye hain unhe fekne ke bajaye zarooratmand ko dein.

💡 Myths vs Facts

Myth (Galat Fahami) ❌ Fact (Sacchai) ✅
Myth: "Plastic kabhi recycle nahi ho sakta, sab kachra zameen mein hi jaata hai." Fact: Agar plastic saaf aur sukha ho, toh ise aasani se pighla kar nayi bottles, kapda aur benches banaye jaate hain.
Myth: "Kaanch ko dobara recycle karne se uski quality gir jaati hai." Fact: Kaanch ki quality 100% same rehti hai; isme bilkul kami nahi aati chahe ise kitni baar bhi pighlaya jaye.
🧑‍🏫 Teacher & Parent Note

Ek purani plastic bottle ya delivery box ghar pe kisi naye kaam mein use karo — jaise usme paudha lagao, stationary holder ya toy container banao! Bachon ko creative reuse (Upcycling) ka anubhav karayein.

📝 Recycling Hero Quiz

5 sawaalon ke sahi uttar de kar apna Recycling Hero badge haasil karein!

1. Kaanch (Glass) recycle hone ke baad kaisa banta hai?
💡 Explanation: Kaanch ko baar-baar recycle karne par bhi uski quality aur chamak mein bilkul kami nahi aati.
2. Plastic recycle karne se pehle sabse zaroori kya hai?
💡 Explanation: Plastic ka saaf hona sabse zaroori hai. Tel ya khane se contaminated plastic recycle nahi ho sakta.
3. Inme se kaun sa dry waste (sukha kachra) hai?
💡 Explanation: Newspaper dry waste hai jise pulp banakar naya kaagaz banaya jaata hai.
4. 3R hierarchy mein sabse pehla aur sabse zaroori step kaun sa hai?
💡 Explanation: 3R mein sabse pehla niyam REDUCE hai — kachra paida hi kam karna sabse behtar paryavaran suraksha hai.
5. Kaagaz recycle karne se sabse badi kya bachat hoti hai?
💡 Explanation: 1 ton paper recycle karne se 17 bade ped aur 26,000 litre se zyada taaza paani bachta hai.
